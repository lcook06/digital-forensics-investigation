# Case 001 - Suspected Insider Data Theft
## Case Information
**Case ID:** 001
**Case Name:** Suspected Insider Data Theft
**Organization:** Starcat Technology Solutions
**Investigator:** Leiawyn Cook
**Status:** Investigation in progress

## Background
Starcat Technology Solutions is a fictional technology company that provides
managed IT services to small and medium-sized businesses.

The company's management became concerned after an employee announced their
intention to leave the organization. Shortly before the employee's departure,
the organization identified unusual activity involving confidential company
documents.

Management believes that company information may have been copied from the
employee's assigned Windows workstation to an external USB storage device.

The organization has requested a digital forensic examination of the workstation
to determine whether unauthorized copying or removal of company information
occurred.

## Suspected Employee
**Name:** John Smith
**Position:** Systems Support Specialist
**Department:** Information Technology

John Smith has access to multiple directories containing internal company documents as part of their normal job responsibilities.

## Investigator Assignment
The investigator has been tasked with examining a forensic image of John Smith's assigned Windows workstation.

The investigation will attempt to determine:

1. Whether an external USB storage device was connected to the workstation.
2. When the device was connected and used.
3. Which user account was active during the relevant activity.
4. Whether company files were accessed or modified.
5. Whether files were copied, deleted, or otherwise manipulated.
6. Whether the available evidence supports a connection between the employee
   and the suspected activity.
7. Whether a timeline of relevant events can be established.

## Scope
The investigation is limited to the provided forensic evidence associated with
the assigned Windows workstation.

The investigation will focus on:

- Windows system artifacts
- User activity
- File system artifacts
- USB device artifacts
- Registry artifacts
- Browser artifacts
- Deleted files
- File metadata
- Relevant timestamps
- Other artifacts discovered during examination

## Investigation Status
The investigation is currently in progress. 
No conclusions will be made until the available evidence has been examined and correlated.
